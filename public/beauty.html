Beauty Ritual

The Ritual

A Maison Vell manicure is unhurried. Four steps, ten minutes, and a finish that holds its shine for a week or more.

01

Prepare

Shape with a glass file, always in one direction. Soften cuticles in warm water, push back gently, and buff the nail plate until it is smooth and matte. Colour holds best on a calm surface.

02

Base

One thin layer of protective base coat, edge to edge, sealing the free edge of the nail. Let it dry for a full two minutes — patience here is what prevents staining and chipping later.

03

Colour

Two thin coats of lacquer, never one thick one. Place the brush a breath away from the cuticle, push toward it, then draw to the tip in a single stroke. Three strokes per nail; five minutes between coats.

04

Seal

Finish with the high-shine top coat, wrapping the free edge. Refresh it every two to three days and the manicure keeps its glass finish for a week or more.
