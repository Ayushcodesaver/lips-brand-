Our World

The Maison

Founded in Paris in 1987, Maison Vell Beauty began as a small atelier devoted to a single idea: that colour, mixed by hand and worn on the fingertips, could be as considered as couture.

The Atelier

Every Maison Vell shade begins as a pigment study on paper, mixed by hand in our Paris atelier. Colourists work in small batches, resting each lacquer for seventy-two hours so the pigment settles into its true depth before a single bottle is filled.

The collection is deliberately narrow. We release few shades and keep them for years, believing that a signature colour — like a signature scent — is found, not chased.

The Formula

Our lacquer is twenty-one free: formulated without the twenty-one ingredients most commonly questioned in nail colour, without compromising shine, coverage or wear.

Each bottle is glass, weighted to sit low in the hand, with a flat precision brush cut to follow the curve of the nail in a single stroke.

Craft

Small batches, hand-mixed pigment and a seventy-two hour rest. Nothing leaves the atelier until the colour is exactly itself.

Colour

Six signature shades, chosen to endure. Depth over novelty, quiet luxury over trend.

Care

A twenty-one free formula, cruelty free from the first batch, in recyclable glass with minimal packaging.
